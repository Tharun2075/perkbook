### Perkbook
A private list of your loyalty cards and the email or phone you used for each one. Add member number, points, expiry, login page and notes; view them grouped by email. No account needed: data stays in your browser's local storage, with free CSV/JSON export. First 5 cards free, then a one-off £6.
